# Dynamic Bayesian Persuasion with Partial Commitment

**Hanshuo Ma** — July 2026. McMaster University, Department of Economics.
Instructor: Prof. Gajendran Raveendranathan.

How much credibility does honest disclosure from a central bank actually need?

Modern central banks operate under announced, transparent frameworks — inflation targets, policy
rules, forward guidance — which amount to a commitment to an information policy in the sense of
Kamenica and Gentzkow (2011). What gives such an announcement bite is that household spending is
belief-driven; what makes it fragile is that the same communication moves the exchange rate. The
commitment is real but imperfect, enforced by institutions and reputation rather than contract.

This paper relaxes full commitment inside a *dynamic* rather than one-shot model, using a
stochastic-commitment friction in which the announced rule is honoured with probability λ.
